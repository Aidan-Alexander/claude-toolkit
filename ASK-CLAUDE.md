# Ask your Claude

No time to look through this site yourself? Paste this into Claude, in a chat or in Claude Code, and it'll read the site, think about how you work, and suggest the tools most likely to help you.

```
Read Aidan Alexander's Claude toolkit at https://aidan-alexander.com/claude-tooling (the same write-ups are on GitHub at https://github.com/Aidan-Alexander/claude-toolkit, if that's easier for you to read). Using what you know about me and my work (my role, the apps I use, what I spend my time on, and anything from our past conversations), tell me which of its tools would be a good fit for my workflow, most useful first, with a sentence on why for each. Skip the ones that wouldn't help me. For each one you recommend, say what I'd need first (for example a connector, or Claude Code) and give me its one-line setup to paste into Claude Code.
```

It works best when your Claude already knows a bit about you, for example from its memory or your past chats.
